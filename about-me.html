About Me

Studying Biology

Hello World! My name is Pedro. I am interested in various subjects such as physics, biology, computer science, philosophy the list goes on. I recently graduated from UC Berkeley with bachelor’s degree in Physics. I am currently attending West Los Angeles College in order to brush up on biology. On a personal note, the reason I am pursuing biology is because I think it is the most rewarding field in all of science, but it is almost certainly the most complex of all science as well. Why you may ask? There are so many reasons. One of the core reasons is evolution. Evolution is dynamic, not static and, as a result, things change consistently at the micro level, which in turn affects the macro level. Following up on the notion of change, DNA recombination, where certain alleles or nucleotides mix or combine between parents, can cause a loss of genetic material and, as a result, certain non-phenotypic traits disappear, which also affects the dynamics of life. If you take all of this into account, it is strange and fascinating how life can do all this, all for the single reason of genetic survival. Then there is the diversity in our biosphere or earth. I think this subject, in particular, is taken for granted since climate change is causing a lot of changes that directly affect not just us humans but other species on our planet as well. Furthermore, understanding how our anatomy and physiology interacts with the world is also captivating. The reason why is because, as humans, we have the ability to manipulate the building blocks of life, so in more than one way it is a gift that we have. I digress; these are just a fraction of the complexities in biology.
